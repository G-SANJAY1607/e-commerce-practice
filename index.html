Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom interior commissions?

Yes, our design atelier accepts private commissions for custom stoneware, handcrafted furniture, and tailored linen collections.

What is your global shipping timeframe?

Complimentary white-glove shipping is available globally. Delivery typically takes 2–4 business days with direct concierge tracking.

How can I schedule a private showroom visit?

Select 'Bespoke Interior Consultation' in our form or send an email to concierge@morrowgoods.com to reserve a private appointment.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We provide a 30-day effortless return window for all standard products. Customized bespoke items are tailored to your specifications.
